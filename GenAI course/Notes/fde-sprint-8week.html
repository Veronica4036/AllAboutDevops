Read this before starting. Compressing 16 weeks into 8 costs two things, and pretending otherwise would set you up to fail. First, the load roughly doubles: 1.5h mornings + 2.5h evenings + 8h Saturday + 6h Sunday ≈ 34 h/week, on top of a full-time on-site engagement. Second, scope is genuinely cut — Spark, dbt and the third project are gone, which means deprioritising Palantir FDSE and Databricks-style data-heavy FDE roles in favour of AI-native ones. Everything tied to your measured gaps (evals, security, agents, system design) survives intact. Open the table below for the full ledger of what was kept, compressed and cut.
